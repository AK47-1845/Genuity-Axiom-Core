# Axiom Product and Commercial Playbook

## Sell a decision outcome

Do not sell rows, a model catalogue, or “AI transformation.” Sell one measurable result: fill a named validation blind spot faster or more safely than collection, manual test-data construction, or bespoke simulation.

## Qualification score

Score each opportunity 0–2 on pain, urgency, economic owner, data/simulator access, independent validation truth, domain validator, measurable baseline, repeatability, private-deployment feasibility and paid-next-step willingness. Pursue 15+/20, discover further at 10–14, decline below 10. A famous logo without access or buyer is not a design partner.

## Initial offer

An 8–12 week paid Scenario Coverage Pilot:

1. one model/software validation decision;
2. one scenario family and modality boundary;
3. source/holdout/intended-use contract;
4. baseline against today’s method;
5. scenario pack and Axiom evidence bundle;
6. downstream real-holdout or simulator evaluation;
7. value and scale recommendation.

Exclude production deployment, safety/regulatory certification, unlimited modalities and unbounded custom simulation unless separately scoped.

## Commercial metrics

- time from request to approved data;
- engineering/simulation/collection hours avoided;
- rare-scenario coverage added;
- model/test performance on independent real evidence;
- false alarms/rejects and reviewer workload;
- repeatability on second asset/configuration;
- conversion to paid next phase and annual platform.

## Pricing logic

Price early pilots by scoped decision and delivery effort. Transition to annual private-runner/platform pricing with limits by programs, protected environments, assets or scenario volume. Charge separately for advanced adapters, domain packs, formal privacy work, multimodal simulator integration and enterprise support. Do not anchor the core business to cost per row.

## Product tiers

- **Axiom Core:** local SDK/CLI, native tabular and evidence baselines.
- **Axiom Design Partner:** guided scenario pack, connector and acceptance scorecard.
- **Axiom Enterprise:** private runner, APIs, identity/security, approvals, registry, monitoring and support—roadmap until built and independently verified.
- **Axiom Scenario OS:** multimodal/simulator orchestration and continuous coverage—long-term direction, not a current SKU.

## Sales proof order

Paid repeat use > buyer-backed design partnership > independent downstream result > replicated POC > expert review > fidelity benchmark > demo > incubator/award. Describe each relationship and POC status exactly.

## Expansion test

Before building a new modality, require at least two opportunities sharing the buyer, workflow, connector, acceptance metrics and deployment boundary. Otherwise partner through the adapter interface. Own scenario/evidence workflow; rent replaceable generation capacity.

## Why an enterprise could eventually pay a high contract value

Only when Axiom becomes embedded across multiple costly validation programs, runs in the required security boundary, reduces measurable cycle cost/risk, and produces evidence accepted by engineering, privacy, safety and procurement. Repository breadth alone does not justify a million-dollar price or valuation.

## Red flags

Unpaid POC without buyer; no independent holdout; user asks only for “more data”; domain expert unavailable; customer forbids evidence access; output cannot enter a real workflow; bespoke work cannot repeat; safety/compliance promise requested from synthetic data alone; world-model language without an operational decision.
