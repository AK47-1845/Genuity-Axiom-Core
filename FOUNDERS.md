# Genuity Axiom — Founder Entry Point

## What the company is

Genuity builds **evidence-gated rare-scenario data infrastructure for industrial AI**. Axiom helps an engineering team define a missing operating condition, generate candidate data through a local model or approved simulator, validate privacy and domain rules, test usefulness on independent evidence, and package an auditable release.

Synthetic data is the current mechanism. Rare-scenario coverage is the customer outcome. Industrial world-model infrastructure is a possible expansion after repeated deployments—not the opening claim.

## Daily decision rule

If a request has no named decision, accountable validator or evaluation truth, do discovery first. If the modality is not native/baseline in `CapabilityRegistry`, use a policy-approved adapter. If any hard release check fails, do not share or operationalize the artifact. If the problem does not repeat or lead to payment, do not expand the platform for it.

## Honest current state

Native local tabular synthesis/evaluation is executable. Relational, time-series, rare-event and unstructured support are safe baselines. Graph/simulation/multimodal are validation contracts. Image/audio/video generation and advanced neural/formal-DP models require approved adapters. The SDK is not yet a secure multi-tenant enterprise service or legal/safety certification product.

## Founder standard

Do not optimize for a grand category description. Optimize until a skeptical customer can answer: Which costly blind spot did Axiom cover? Why are these scenarios credible? Are they safe for this recipient and purpose? Did they improve a real decision? Will the customer pay to repeat it?
